KRINIK TRIBIT

The Tribit app with fixes: no account, no Firebase, no location.

1Connecting a speaker

  1. Turn on the speaker and Bluetooth on your phone.
  2. Open Krinik Tribit and tap “START”.
  3. Allow access to nearby devices: the phone needs it to find the speaker over Bluetooth. The app does not ask for your location.
  4. On the home screen, tap “Tap to Add Device” and pick your speaker.

2Equalizer

On the speaker page, open “Sound Effects”. To create your own equalizer, tap “New Custom”. An equalizer name holds up to 15 characters.

The “Device Name” row of the speaker settings (the gear on the speaker page) renames the speaker, up to 25 characters.

3Speaker firmware

The app checks the firmware when you tap the “Firmware Version” row of the speaker settings. It then asks the Tribit server whether there is new firmware for this model and shows the answer.

4Privacy

Krinik Tribit respects your privacy and the GDPR. The Tribit server cannot tell who you are.

A guest is a sign-in to the Tribit server with no account and no personal data. The app works like this:

  • there is no account: the app signs in to the Tribit server as an anonymous guest;
  • the guest ID, the phone model and the Android version in its requests are random: the app picks them once and keeps them until the app's data is cleared;
  • only four kinds of requests reach the Tribit server: the guest sign-in, the speaker catalog, the firmware check and the translations of the interface;
  • Firebase (analytics with the advertising ID, Crashlytics, push messages) is removed from the app.

5A new anonymous guest

To sign in to the Tribit server as another guest, clear the app's data in Android. The app then picks a new random guest ID, phone model and Android version.

Settings → Apps → Krinik Tribit → Storage → Clear data

After a reinstall the app also signs in as another guest.
